# Product vision

## What it is

Plotcraft is a desktop tool for architecting stories. Its purpose is to help authors organize and develop a story as a cohesive project, from the first concept to the writing.

That is why Plotcraft should not be thought of only as a text editor. Writing matters, but it happens inside a larger space that also gives the story context and lets you organize its development.

## Core experience

When the app opens, the person should find a projects area to start a new project or continue an existing one. Entering a project should lead to that story's workspace. This separation gives the project a clear place before any specific writing tool.

The project is the central structure of the product. It identifies the story, presents its context and gathers the writing, the gallery and the narrative elements. A book project can keep the text continuous or split it into chapters.

## Principles

1. **Stories as projects** — every story deserves its own identity, context and organization.
2. **Architecture alongside writing** — support planning and development, not only drafting.
3. **Desktop first** — design the main experience for computers and their wide screens.
4. **Customizable authoring** — allow custom themes and an interface available in more than one language.

## Status

This is the intended direction for the product. The desktop projects area lets you create and open stories. The workspace brings together an overview, rich-text writing, a gallery and narrative elements. Mentions in the text link the writing to the project's elements.
